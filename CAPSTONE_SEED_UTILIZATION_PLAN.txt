SECTION 2: HARVEST WEIGHT STANDARDS
================================================================================

Fresh Weight:
  Definition: Wet paddy (rice) as harvested directly from field
  Moisture Level: ~25% moisture content
  When Recorded: At harvest time by BRGY President
  Example: 1,800 kg fresh from field

Dry Weight:
  Definition: After drying to 14% moisture (DA standard)
  Moisture Level: 14% moisture content
  When Used: For all DA yield comparisons and system calculations
  Conversion: Fresh → Dry = deduct 10-15% (use 12% as default)
  Formula: Dry Weight = Fresh Weight × 0.88 (12% reduction)
  Example: 1,800 kg × 0.88 = 1,584 kg dry weight

CRITICAL: DA target yields (e.g., 4,000 kg/ha for HYBRID) are in DRY WEIGHT
  If system uses fresh weight without conversion, calculations will be wrong!

UI Implementation:
  → BrgyHarvest form must have toggle: [Fresh Weight] or [Dried Weight]
  → If Fresh selected: System auto-converts to dry weight for calculations
  → Display both values: "Harvest: 1,800 kg (fresh) = 1,584 kg (dry weight)"

SECTION 4: AUTOMATIC CALCULATIONS (System Computes These)
================================================================================

CALCULATION 1: Harvest in Kilograms
  harvest_kg = harvest_bags × 50
  Example: 36 bags × 50 = 1,800 kg fresh
  With dry weight: 1,800 kg × 0.88 = 1,584 kg dry
  Display: "Harvest: 1,800 kg (fresh) = 1,584 kg (dry weight)"

---

CALCULATION 2: Standard Expected Yield
  Used to compare farmer performance
  Formula: Area Planted (ha) × Standard Yield per Seed Type
  
  For HYBRID:
    Expected = 0.3 ha × 4,000 kg/ha = 1,200 kg
  
  For INBRED:
    Expected = 0.3 ha × 1,500 kg/ha = 450 kg
  
  For OWN_SEED:
    Expected = 0.3 ha × 2,000 kg/ha = 600 kg
  
  Note: This uses AREA ACTUALLY PLANTED (0.3 ha), not planned (0.5 ha)

---

CALCULATION 3: Seed Implied Planted (NEW - CRITICAL)
  This is how much seed farmer actually needed
  Formula: Area Planted (ha) × Seeding Density (kg/ha)
  
  For HYBRID:
    Implied = 0.3 ha × 15 kg/ha = 4.5 kg
  
  For INBRED:
    Implied = 0.3 ha × 40 kg/ha = 12 kg
  
  Why This Works:
    - ATs measure area in field (verifiable)
    - System calculates seed from standard density (scientific)
    - Eliminates farmer guessing "I planted about 5 kilos"
    - Protects farmer reputation (not blamed for extra seeds)

---

CALCULATION 4: Seed Efficiency Percentage (NEW)
  How much of distributed seed was actually needed
  Formula: (Seed Implied Planted ÷ Seed Distributed) × 100
  
  Example:
    Seed Implied Planted: 4.5 kg
    Seed Distributed: 15 kg
    Seed Efficiency = (4.5 ÷ 15) × 100 = 30%
  
  Meaning:
    - Only 30% of distributed seed was needed for 0.3 ha
    - 70% was leftover (kept as backup, shared with neighbor, etc.)
    - Government can see if over-supplying certain barangays
    - NOT a criticism of farmer - it's a supply chain metric

---

CALCULATION 5: Harvest Utilization Percentage (MAIN METRIC - NEW LOGIC)
  This is the MOST IMPORTANT metric - shows farmer performance
  Formula: (Actual Harvest ÷ Expected Harvest) × 100
  
  OLD SYSTEM (WRONG):
    Harvest ÷ Seed Distributed × 100 = meaningless number
    Example: 1,800 ÷ 15 = 12,000% (nonsense)
  
  NEW SYSTEM (CORRECT):
    Harvest ÷ (Area Actually Planted × Standard Yield) × 100
    Example: 1,584 (dry) ÷ (0.3 ha × 4,000 kg/ha) × 100
             = 1,584 ÷ 1,200 × 100
             = 132% ✅ EXCELLENT PERFORMANCE
  
  What It Means:
    - 132% means farmer harvested 32% MORE than standard yield
    - This is EXCELLENT performance
    - Without this fix: Would look like farmer failed (75% if using 0.5 ha)
    - With this fix: Shows Juan is a Master Farmer
  
  Interpretation Guide:
    ≥ 100%  → Excellent / Above Target
    80-99%  → Good / Meets Target
    50-79%  → Fair / Below Target (possible pest/disease)
    < 50%   → Poor / Major Problems

---

CALCULATION 6: Productivity Ratio (NEW)
  How much paddy produced per 1 kg of seed planted
  Formula: Harvest ÷ Seed Implied Planted
  
  Example:
    Harvest: 1,584 kg (dry)
    Seed Planted: 4.5 kg
    Productivity = 1,584 ÷ 4.5 = 352 kg paddy per 1 kg seed
  
  Standard HYBRID: 266.67 kg/kg (4,000 kg ÷ 15 kg/ha)
  Juan's Achievement: 352 kg/kg
  Performance: +32% above standard ✅
  
  What It Means:
    - Each kg of seed produced 352 kg paddy (excellent efficiency)
    - Can compare across farmers and seasons
    - Shows seed-to-harvest conversion rate

SECTION 6: CAPSTONE DEFENSE TALKING POINTS
================================================================================

Opening Statement:
"We recognized that agricultural seed utilization calculations need to be based
on verifiable field measurements, not guesswork. Our system links all five 
phases of the rice farming cycle to create an accurate, defensible picture of 
farmer performance."

Problem Solved:
"Traditional systems calculate utilization from seed distributed. This is wrong
because it ignores the actual farm area. A farmer who plants 0.3 ha and achieves
high yield looks like a failure when compared to 0.5 ha planned area. Our system
fixes this by using area ACTUALLY planted (measured by AT) to calculate real 
farm productivity."

Technical Innovation:
"We use 'Implied Seed Usage' - a scientific calculation based on standard 
seeding density (15 kg/ha for HYBRID, 40 kg/ha for INBRED). The AT doesn't 
need to weigh seeds in the field. They measure land area, and our system 
calculates how much seed should have been planted. This is infinitely more 
reliable than asking a farmer to guess kilogram amounts."

Real-World Impact (Using Example):
"Juan received 15 kg seed, but planted only 0.3 ha due to water shortage.
- Old system: 1,800 kg ÷ 15 kg = meaningless (12,000%)
- Our system: 1,584 kg ÷ (0.3 ha × 4,000 kg/ha) = 132% EXCELLENT
  This correctly shows Juan as an excellent farmer, not a failure."

Data Architecture Validity:
"Our system is based on real Department of Agriculture standards:
- Seeding density constants (HYBRID 15 kg/ha, INBRED 40 kg/ha)
- Dry weight standards for yield comparison (14% moisture)
- AT measurement protocols (area in hectares)
- This design is fully defensible for production deployment."

Fresh vs Dry Weight Handling:
"We added a toggle for Fresh/Dry weight because DA yield targets are in dry 
weight (14% moisture), but BRGY records fresh weight (25% moisture). The system
automatically converts 1,800 kg fresh → 1,584 kg dry for fair comparison."

Scalability:
"This system works for any barangay because:
1. Area is measured by AT (same everywhere)
2. Seeding constants are DA standards (same everywhere)
3. Formula uses verified science, not local assumptions
4. Can be deployed as is to any municipality in Philippines"

SECTION 8: KEY REMINDERS & WARNINGS
================================================================================

CRITICAL POINTS:
⚠️  PSB Rc 22 is INBRED (40 kg/ha), NOT HYBRID (15 kg/ha)
⚠️  Always use DRY WEIGHT for yield calculations (14% moisture standard)
⚠️  Never ask AT for "actual kg planted" - calculate from area instead
⚠️  HYBRID standard yield is 4,000 kg/ha (dry weight), not 3,500 or 4,500
⚠️  INBRED standard yield is 1,500 kg/ha (dry weight)
⚠️  OWN_SEED standard yield = ❓ CONFIRM WITH CLIENT (currently 2,000 kg/ha)
⚠️  Fresh to dry conversion should be ~12% (10-15% range acceptable)
⚠️  ONE farmer creates MULTIPLE HarvestRecords (one per seed type grown)
⚠️  Each HarvestRecord calculated independently using seed type's standard yield

COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID:
❌ Using HYBRID density (15 kg/ha) for INBRED seeds
❌ Comparing fresh weight harvest to dry weight targets
❌ Calculating utilization from seed distributed (use area × standard instead)
❌ Asking farmer to report "kg planted" instead of letting system calculate
❌ Using planned area (0.5 ha) instead of actually planted area (0.3 ha)
❌ Forgetting to link harvest record to distribution entry
❌ Creating one HarvestRecord for total harvest (should be one per seed type)
❌ Using OWN_SEED standard yield without client confirmation
❌ Mixing seed types in one entry (keep them separate)

BENEFITS OF THIS SYSTEM:
✅ Protects farmers from looking inefficient due to supply padding
✅ Shows real farm productivity per seed type (220% HYBRID vs 117% INBRED, etc.)
✅ Uses scientifically verified constants (not local guesses)
✅ Based on AT's actual field measurements (verifiable)
✅ Separates performance by seed type (fair comparison)
✅ Scalable to any barangay (same calculation everywhere)
✅ Defensible in capstone oral defense
