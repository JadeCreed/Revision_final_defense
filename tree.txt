The reason maxZoom: 13 was causing the zoom-out issue is that it was forcing a cap, but if the computed fitBounds zoom is already 13 or higher, capping at 13 is fine — the problem is that the padding of [30, 30] combined with the container size was computing a zoom of 12 or lower. Removing the cap and reducing padding to [20, 20] lets Leaflet naturally fit Lucban as tightly as possible in the available container.
